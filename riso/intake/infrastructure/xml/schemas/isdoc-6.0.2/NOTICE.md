# ISDOC 6.0.2 schema

`isdoc-invoice-6.0.2.xsd` is the unmodified ISDOC invoice schema, version 6.0.2 of
23 March 2022, downloaded on 2026-11-11 from <https://isdoc.cz/6.0.2/xsd/isdoc-invoice-6.0.2.xsd>
(the current version listed at <https://archiv.mv.gov.cz/isdoc/clanek/aktualni-verze.aspx>).
riso validates incoming ISDOC invoices, and ISDOC files attached to PDFs, against it
(plan §8.4).
