# OASIS Universal Business Language (UBL) 2.1 schemas

The files under `maindoc/` and `common/` are the unmodified XML Schemas of UBL 2.1, OASIS
Standard of 4 November 2013 (also ISO/IEC 19845:2015), downloaded on 2026-11-11 from
<http://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.1/xsd/>. Only the Invoice and CreditNote document
schemas and the common modules they import are included. riso validates incoming
Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 invoices and credit notes against them (plan §8.4).
